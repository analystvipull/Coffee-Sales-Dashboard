
# ☕ Coffee Store Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

☕ **Coffee Store Sales Analytics Dashboard**

A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze coffee sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, revenue trends, and transaction patterns across different time periods.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Coffee Store Sales Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report developed to monitor coffee shop performance through sales, revenue, product popularity, and customer purchasing trends. The dashboard helps business owners and analysts identify top-selling beverages, peak sales periods, and revenue-driving products to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

• 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization platform used for dashboard creation and reporting.

• 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data extraction, cleaning, transformation, and preparation.

• 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for calculated measures, KPIs, and dynamic business calculations.

• 📝 **Data Modeling** – Relationships and business logic created to enable filtering, aggregation, and cross-analysis.

• 📈 **Data Visualization** – KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Donut Charts, Slicers, and Trend Analysis visuals.

• 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for dashboard development and `.png` for dashboard previews.

---

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains transaction-level coffee sales records, including:

* Transaction Date & Time
* Coffee Product Name
* Sales Amount
* Payment Method
* Time of Day
* Day of Week
* Month Information
* Customer Transaction Records

The dataset includes over 3,600 transactions and provides detailed insights into product performance, sales behavior, and revenue generation.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Coffee shops generate hundreds of transactions daily, making it difficult to track sales performance, customer preferences, and product demand using raw transactional data alone.

Key questions include:

* Which coffee products generate the highest revenue?
* What are the busiest sales periods?
* Which products are most popular among customers?
* How does revenue vary across months and weekdays?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* How can management optimize inventory and staffing decisions?

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive visual tool that:

* Monitors coffee store sales performance.
* Tracks revenue and transaction trends.
* Identifies best-selling and underperforming products.
* Analyzes customer purchasing behavior.
* Supports inventory planning and staffing decisions.
* Enables data-driven business growth strategies.

---

### • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

#### Key KPIs (Top Row)

The dashboard provides a quick overview of store performance through key metrics such as:

* Total Revenue
* Total Transactions
* Average Order Value
* Total Products Sold
* Revenue Growth Metrics

#### Date Filter Panel

An interactive date slicer allows users to filter all visuals by selected time periods for customized analysis.

#### Sales Trend Over Time (Line Chart)

Displays revenue trends across months and helps identify seasonal sales patterns and peak-performing periods.

#### Revenue by Product (Bar Chart)

Ranks coffee products based on total revenue generated.

This helps identify the beverages contributing most to business profitability.

#### Product Sales Distribution (Donut Chart)

Shows the proportion of total sales contributed by each coffee category.

Useful for understanding customer preferences and product demand.

#### Sales by Time of Day (Column Chart)

Compares transaction activity across:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* Night

Helps management identify peak operating hours.

#### Sales by Weekday (Bar Chart)

Analyzes purchasing behavior across days of the week to identify high-demand periods.

#### Payment Method Analysis

Displays transaction distribution across payment methods, helping understand customer payment preferences.

#### Top-Selling Products Analysis

Highlights the best-performing coffee products based on sales volume and revenue generation.

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

#### Revenue Optimization

Management can identify the products contributing the highest revenue and prioritize them in promotions and menu strategies.

#### Inventory Planning

Product demand analysis helps ensure sufficient inventory levels while reducing waste and stock shortages.

#### Customer Preference Analysis

Sales distribution across products provides insights into customer tastes and purchasing behavior.

#### Workforce Scheduling

Time-of-day and weekday sales trends help optimize employee scheduling during peak business hours.

#### Marketing Opportunities

Seasonal and product-level insights enable targeted promotional campaigns and personalized customer engagement.

#### Strategic Decision Making

The dashboard consolidates sales, product performance, and customer behavior into a single analytical platform, helping stakeholders make informed business decisions and improve overall profitability.
